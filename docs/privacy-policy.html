PressScribe Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026 · App package: com.konashevich.pressscribe

PressScribe (“the App”) is an Android audio note and transcription tool published by Oleksii Konashevych. This policy explains what data the App handles and how it is used.

1. Summary

PressScribe does not run its own backend for accounts, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting. Audio and text you create are processed either on device (local storage) or by services you choose to configure: Google Gemini and/or a self-hosted speech recognition server.

2. Data the App processes

  • Microphone audio — when you record inside the App (requires microphone permission).
  • Imported or shared audio files — when you import audio or another app shares audio with PressScribe.
  • Transcript and polished text — text generated or edited in the App, including notes you save.
  • Settings you enter — for example your Gemini API key, model name, prompts, listen/volume preferences, and optional self-hosted server host/port/path.

3. Where data is stored

  • On your device: settings (including API key), saved notes, and temporary audio files used for transcription are stored in the App’s local Android storage / sandbox.
  • Not on PressScribe servers: the developer does not operate a PressScribe cloud that receives your audio, transcripts, or API keys.

4. Network transfers you enable

Depending on your settings, the App may send data over the internet:

  • Google Gemini (default option): when Gemini transcription or text polishing/translation is used, audio and/or text are sent to Google’s Gemini API using the API key you provide. Google’s processing is governed by Google’s terms and privacy policy for the Gemini / Google AI APIs.
  • Self-hosted ASR: when you select a self-hosted transcription service, audio is uploaded to the server address you configure. That server is controlled by you or your organization, not by PressScribe.

If you do not configure a Gemini API key and do not use a self-hosted server, the App will not be able to complete cloud transcription or polishing features.

5. Permissions

  • Microphone — to record audio for transcription.
  • Internet — to call Gemini or your self-hosted ASR endpoint.
  • Vibrate — optional haptic feedback when recording starts/stops.

6. Data we do not collect

PressScribe does not intentionally collect or sell:

  • Advertising IDs or ad profiles
  • Analytics / telemetry SDKs for marketing
  • Account registration data (no PressScribe login)
  • Precise location, contacts, or SMS

7. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App to submit personal information about children to third-party AI services.

8. Your controls

  • Revoke microphone permission in Android system settings.
  • Delete or clear notes and settings inside the App, or clear app storage in Android settings.
  • Remove your Gemini API key from Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App removes local App data from the device (subject to Android backups you may have enabled).

9. Third-party services

  • Google Play (distribution / optional Play services on the device)
  • Google Gemini API (when configured by you)
  • Any self-hosted ASR endpoint you configure

Those providers process data under their own policies when you use them.
